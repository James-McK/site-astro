## Beastieball

Released: 2024, playtime: 26 hours

![alt text](./beastiebol.avif)

It's not a particularly hard game for the most part, but it's a lot of fun thinking about your positioning, when to swap out your beasties (or when to take the risk of not doing so), and trying to set up for some more powerful

My favourite soundtrack of any game I've played this year - I've found myself coming back to it regularly, it's exciting, bouncy, and fun.
Was also a lot of fun playing matches against my girlfriend

## despolote

Released: 2025, playtime: 2.2 hours

![alt text](despolote.avif)

A fascinating and beautiful view into life as Ecuador qualifies for the world cup. Don't dismiss it if you have no interest in football (I definitely don't!) - like a lot of the best games, it's about people.

## If Found

Released: 2020, playtime: 2.4 hours

![alt text](return.avif)

A story about home, and friends, irishness, and life. Alien in some ways and far too relatable in others. I wouldn't have tried to play this again if it weren't for you bug, so thank you <3
